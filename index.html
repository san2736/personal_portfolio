About me

I'm Sanaz, a B.Tech Data Science student at NMIMS MPSTME in Mumbai, on a programme that included a Diploma in Computer Science. I work mostly in Python, and I like the whole journey of a data problem: collecting and cleaning the data, testing it with proper statistics and machine learning, and turning the result into a dashboard someone can actually act on.

My favourite questions have a number for an answer. How much of a free Spotify listener's time is actually music? 74.9%, according to the Markov chain I built on a year of real listening data. Which mutual fund houses get the most investor complaints? I built a dashboard to find out.

Away from the laptop I paint, I sing, and I play chess. I'm a UCMAS mental arithmetic graduate, so I've usually done the sum before the calculator opens. I also spent a month volunteering at the S.V. Phatak Library at Lokmanya Seva Sangh, digitising 339 rare English books. And on race weekends, the timing screen is the most important window on my desktop.

  2. P5

    Spotify Listening Markov Chain

    74.9% music, 21% ads

    I modelled a year of real Spotify listening data (24,977 plays) as a four-state Markov chain: library, discovery, ad and offline. Its stationary distribution shows a free-tier listener spends 74.9% of their time on music and 21% on ads, and doubling the ads would cut music time by 9.3 points.

    • JavaScript
    • Chart.js
    • Markov chains
