About me

I'm Sanaz, a B.Tech Data Science student at NMIMS MPSTME in Mumbai, with a Diploma in Computer Science already behind me. I work mostly in Python and SQL, and I enjoy every stage of a data problem: designing a database that keeps records honest, cleaning and testing the data with proper statistics, and turning the result into a Power BI or Tableau dashboard that someone can actually act on.

Lately I've been deep in probability, building Markov chain models of real-world trends for my Stochastic Processes course. I also like making tools people use: I led the team behind Find Your Faculty, a campus web app that tells students where their professors are and when they're free.

Away from the laptop I paint, I sing, and I play chess. I'm also a UCMAS mental arithmetic graduate, so I will usually have done the sum before the calculator opens. And on race weekends, the timing screen is the most important window on my desktop.
